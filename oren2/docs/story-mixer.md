# Story script: the chase after the Mixer (spec only, #385)

One continuing, funny story for the day's path. Oren and his friend (the pet he chose, called
הֶחָבֵר here) sail after **the Mixer — הַמְּבַלְבֵּל**: a small, giggly, harmless character who
sneezes, laughs and mixes things up (numbers, clocks, letters, recipes, socks). He is never
scary and never mean; in chapter 11 we learn he just wanted someone to play with, and in
chapter 12 he joins the crew. All characters are original.

## What grows on the home screen: the ship (owner answer 2)

The one place that grows on the home screen is **the ship — הַסְּפִינָה**. Nothing else grows.
Every finished scene adds one piece to the ship, so after 60 scenes the ship is complete.

- One chapter = one part of the ship (5 pieces). One scene = one step of the day's path.
- The piece Oren earns is the item in the scene's last column; it appears on the ship right
  after the scene's closing line.
- Order of the parts: 1 hull, 2 deck, 3 mast, 4 sail, 5 steering, 6 anchor and oars,
  7 cabin outside, 8 night lights, 9 cabin inside, 10 deck extras, 11 decorations, 12 party.

## How to read the tables

- **Open** — the friend's opening line before the step: why we do this.
- **Close** — the line on the result card after the step: what changed.
- **Topic** — the matching topic id of the topic picker (`oren2/topic.js` TOPICS):
  `add` חיבור, `sub` חיסור, `mul` כפל, `div` חילוק, `time` שעון, `read` קריאה, `eng` אנגלית, `think` חידות.
- **Item** — the ship piece Oren earns (kid text with nikud, English in brackets).
- Kid lines: short Hebrew with nikud, at most 2 sentences each.
